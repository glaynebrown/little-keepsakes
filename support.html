Getting started

Do I need to make an account?

No. Little Keepsakes uses the Apple Account already on your iPhone. There’s no email or password to set up. Everything you add saves to your own iCloud automatically.

Getting everything on a new iPhone or my iPad

Sign in to the same Apple Account, install Little Keepsakes and open it. It looks in your iCloud first and brings everything back, which can take a minute if you have lots of photos.

Settings says it isn’t saving to iCloud

Open the Settings app and make sure you’re signed in to your Apple Account with iCloud turned on. If your iCloud storage is full, new keepsakes stay on your iPhone until you free up space or add more. Settings → iCloud in Little Keepsakes shows what’s happening.

Bringing in a backup

On the first screen, tap Have a backup? Bring it in (or Settings → Your data → Import a backup while your family is still empty) and pick the backup’s .zip file. Keep the app open until it says it’s done. If it gets interrupted, pick the same file again to finish.

Family sharing

Inviting my partner, grandparents or others

Settings → Family sharing → Invite people. Choose Can make changes for someone who adds moments too, or View only for someone who just wants to see the timeline. Send the invite by Messages or Mail. Only the person you invite can use their link. A family can have up to 6 people besides you.

Changing what someone can do, or removing them

Settings → Family sharing → Invite or manage people, then tap their name. Someone who left, or hasn’t joined yet, stays invited until you remove them.

Joining a family

Tap the invite link on your iPhone with Little Keepsakes installed and choose Join. You can be in one family at a time. If you have your own family in the app, it stays saved in your iCloud and comes back if you leave.

Leaving a family

Settings → Family → Leave. Their keepsakes are removed from your iPhone, and they can invite you again anytime.

Free version and unlock

What does the free version include?

Up to 5 moments, 5 firsts, 5 words and 5 saved things to do, with every feature. Right Now snapshots, letters, growth, the wheel, PDF books and backups are always free. Unlock everything is a one-time purchase. No subscription and no ads.

I bought the unlock but it’s not showing

Open the unlock screen and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you’re signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with. If you use Apple Family Sharing, people in your Apple family get it too.

Does everyone I invite need to buy it?

No. People you invite get whatever your family has: if you’ve unlocked it, so have they.

More

Adding widgets

Press and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget and search for Keepsakes. Choose Photo frame, Birthday countdown, What should we do today? or Quick add. Press and hold a widget and tap Edit Widget to pick which child or photos it shows.

Opening places in Google Maps instead of Apple Maps

Settings → Home → Open places in → Google Maps. If Google Maps isn’t installed, Apple Maps opens instead.

Saving a copy of everything

Settings → Your data → Export a backup saves one file with every photo and everything you wrote. Save it to Files or iCloud Drive. Books makes a PDF album you can print.

Deleting everything

Settings → Your data → Delete everything. This erases your family’s keepsakes from your iPhone and your iCloud, and everyone you invited loses access. Photos still in your phone’s photo library aren’t touched. Make a backup first if you’d like a copy.
