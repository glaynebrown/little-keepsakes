Privacy Policy

Little Keepsakes · effective October 6, 2026

The short version

  • I never receive your family’s information. There are no accounts and no servers of mine. Everything you add is kept on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.
  • No ads. No tracking. No analytics. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • You can save a full copy of everything, or delete all of it, at any time from inside the app.

Little Keepsakes is made by Gabriella Brown, an individual developer (“I” below). Questions? Email keepsakeapps@gmail.com.

What the app keeps, and where

What you add (your kids’ names and birthdays, moments, photos, videos, firsts, quotes, words, letters, growth, snapshots, saved things to do and your settings) is saved on your iPhone first. It is then copied to your own iCloud account using Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit), so it comes back on a new phone and shows up on your other Apple devices.

  • It’s stored in your iCloud, not with me. I can’t see it, and it counts toward your own iCloud storage.
  • The words you write are stored in encrypted fields, and photos are stored encrypted by Apple. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • If your iPhone isn’t signed in to iCloud, everything stays on your iPhone only.

Photos and the camera

Little Keepsakes only gets the photos you pick, or a picture you take with the camera from inside the app. It doesn’t look through the rest of your photo library. Photos are made smaller on your iPhone before they’re saved, and the originals in your library are never changed or deleted.

Sharing with family

If you invite someone (your partner, grandparents and so on), the invite goes through Apple’s iCloud sharing. People you invite can see your family’s keepsakes:

  • Can make changes: they see and add to everything, including letters.
  • View only: they see the timeline (moments, photos, growth and words), but not letters, things to do, books or backups.

Apple shows the people in a shared family each other’s names. The name someone picks for “Added by” is saved with your family’s keepsakes. You can change what each person can do, or remove them, at any time, and anyone can leave a family whenever they like.

Things to do and maps

  • Place search: when you search for places, what you type and your home town are sent to Apple Maps to find places and drive times. Apple says Maps searches aren’t tied to your Apple Account.
  • Directions: tapping Maps or Directions opens Apple Maps, or Google Maps if you choose it in Settings, with that place. What happens there is covered by Apple’s or Google’s privacy policy.
  • Links you save (like a TikTok idea) open in your browser only when you tap them.
  • Little Keepsakes doesn’t use your iPhone’s location. Drive times start from the home town you type in.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Widgets get a small copy of a few photos, birthdays and ideas, kept on your iPhone only.
  • Backups and PDF books you make are saved wherever you choose, like Files or iCloud Drive. They never go to me.
  • Importing a backup reads the file on your iPhone and adds what’s in it to your family.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is bought through Apple. The app only asks Apple whether this Apple Account has unlocked it. I never receive your name, email or payment details. In a shared family, the owner’s phone saves whether the family is unlocked, so the people they invite get the same.

Your choices

  • Keep a copy: Settings → Your data → Export a backup, or Keepsakes & export → Books for a PDF.
  • Change things: edit or delete anything you’ve added, any time.
  • Sharing: Settings → Family sharing to invite, change or remove people. Someone in your family can leave from the same place.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Your data → Delete everything. This erases your family’s keepsakes from your iPhone and your iCloud, and everyone you invited loses access.

If you’d like help with any of this, email keepsakeapps@gmail.com.

Children

Little Keepsakes is made for parents and family to keep memories of their children. It isn’t meant to be used by children themselves, and since I never receive anyone’s information, I don’t collect any from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Big changes will also be mentioned in the app.
